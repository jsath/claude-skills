# Hooks and natural voice (load before writing any script)

## The audience test
Write for one named viewer from PROFILE.md (for example "a busy owner who tried AI and could not get it working").
Every line has to make sense to that person said across a table.

## A good hook does three things
1. Calls out the right person or the exact problem.
2. Opens a question they need answered.
3. Sounds like something a person would actually say out loud.

## The 9 hook types
| Type | Shape | Example shape (fill with your own facts) |
|---|---|---|
| Callout | names the viewer or their situation | "If you still do X by hand every month, watch this." |
| Mistake / warning | the thing they are doing wrong | "Stop installing X until you check this one thing." |
| Belief flip | the common belief is wrong | "Everyone thinks X. It is actually Y." |
| Proof | a result you can show | "I ran X on my own Y. Here is what came back." |
| Number | a specific, checkable number | "This free repo has N stars and does X." |
| Story opener | drops into a moment | "Last week a client asked me X." |
| Question | a question they have asked themselves | "Why does X keep happening to you?" |
| Curiosity gap | something hidden or little known | "There is a hidden X in Y that most people never use." |
| Comparison / POV | this versus that | "Paid tool X versus free tool Y. Same job." |

**Write 3 hooks per script, each a different type.** All three get recorded and each ships as its own trial reel;
the scorecard decides which hook wins. Keep each hook 8 to 16 words, said inside 1.5 to 4 s.

## Natural voice rules
1. Keep joining words (but, so, and, well, honestly).
2. Say it the long, simple way. No compressed clever lines.
3. Tell stories in full sentences, not notes.
4. One idea per video. Go deep with one real example.
5. Say the payoff plainly.
6. No punchline endings, quote lines, rhymes or three-word rhythms. If it belongs on a mug, cut it.
7. No hype words: leverage, unlock, game changer, revolutionise, 10x. Specifics beat excitement.
8. FaceTime test: would you say it like this to a friend on a call?
9. Only say what you can back up. Anything unsure goes in [brackets] to confirm or cut before filming.

## Structure (short video)
Hook -> why it matters (the real problem) -> the one idea, with a real example -> proof (something you did) ->
a simple close, then the CTA ending.

## Before recording
Read it aloud once. Change anything that feels weird to say. Fill or cut every [bracket]. Record all 3 hooks.
Flag any number that sounds wrong.
