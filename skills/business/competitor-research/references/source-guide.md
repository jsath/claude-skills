# Source guide

What each source is good for, what it hides, and the usual label it supports.

| Source | Good for | Watch out for | Usual label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage and product pages | Positioning, module names, who they say it is for | Aspirational copy, features that are really roadmap | VERIFIED (that they say it) |
| Pricing page and plan table | Plan names, what each plan includes, billing terms | Footnotes, "starting at", add-ons, "contact sales" tiers | VERIFIED for published items, UNKNOWN for the rest |
| Docs, help center, API reference | Real limits, quotas, setup steps, plan gates | Docs for old versions; check the date or version | VERIFIED |
| Changelog and release notes | What shipped and when, release pace | Launch posts that say "early access" and are never updated | VERIFIED, with availability caveat |
| Security, trust, privacy, terms | Certifications, data handling, data location | A certification claim is not proof of any specific control | VERIFIED (that they claim it) |
| Integrations or marketplace | Named connectors | A logo is not proof of two-way sync or migration | VERIFIED for the listing, UNKNOWN for depth |
| Case studies and logos | Who has used them, use cases | Hand-picked customers, vendor-written numbers, past relationships | VENDOR-CLAIMED |
| Job posts | Team shape, services depth, segments, tech direction | Intent, not current capability; pooled teams vs dedicated | INFERRED |
| App store listings | Release frequency, ratings, data-safety declarations | Ratings skew; declarations are self-reported | VERIFIED (listing), CORROBORATED (patterns) |
| Public filings | Revenue lines, risk factors, customer counts as defined by them | Definitions differ from marketing numbers | VERIFIED |
| Independent news or analyst coverage | Context, funding, leadership changes, outside views | Rewritten press releases count as vendor voice | CORROBORATED if truly independent |
| Review sites and community forums | Setup pain, support quality, recurring gaps | Small samples, incentivized reviews, very old reviews | CORROBORATED only as a pattern across several |
| Customer's own announcement | Confirms a real relationship and scope | Scope may be one team or one pilot | CORROBORATED |
| Your own call notes and lost-deal reasons | What buyers actually hear and care about | Private; one buyer is not a market | INFERRED, internal use only |

## Quick decision for a label

1. Did the competitor publish it about themselves? Factual feature, plan or policy: **VERIFIED**. Performance, scale or results: **VENDOR-CLAIMED**.
2. Does an independent source confirm it, or do two independent sources agree? **CORROBORATED**.
3. Is it your conclusion from evidence? **INFERRED**, and write down the evidence.
4. Nothing solid, or sources conflict? **UNKNOWN**, and write the question that would settle it.

## Search patterns that help

- `site:<their domain> <feature>` to find docs and help pages the navigation hides.
- `<their domain>/sitemap.xml` to list every public page.
- `"<competitor name>" <feature> review` and `"<competitor name>" vs` for independent views.
- `"<competitor name>" hiring <role>` or their careers page for team shape.
- A public web archive of their pricing page to see how plans changed over time (record both dates).
