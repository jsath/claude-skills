Example (fictional): **Acme Scheduling vs Example CRM**

- In one line: Example CRM is a sales CRM for small teams that added a booking page as one feature among many. (VERIFIED E01, INFERRED E05)
- Strong: one tool for contacts and deals, so no second login. (VERIFIED E01)
- Where we win: their booking page is limited to one calendar on their mid tier (VERIFIED E01); reviewers report setup needs support help (CORROBORATED E04). Raise it as: "How many people need bookable calendars, and can you try that setup in a trial?"
- Can say: "Their pricing page lists booking with one calendar on that plan."
- Must not say: "Their booking feature is in beta" (pages conflict, E01 vs E02), "customers hate it" (four reviews are a pattern, not a verdict), or any enterprise price (UNKNOWN, E06).
